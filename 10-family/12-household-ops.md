# How My Household Runs

---

## How to Use This File

Copy everything inside the prompt block below and paste it into any AI of your choice — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi, or any other. This is the operational layer — less narrative than `11-family.md`, more factual. Where `11-family.md` captures the people and relationships, this file captures the reference data: schedules, contacts, calendar systems, and the practical details an EA needs to manage your logistics. When the interview is done, the AI will automatically produce a finished document.

---

## The Prompt

Click the **copy** button in the top-right corner of the grey box below, then paste it into your AI.

```
[For agents running a vault build session: conduct this interview directly with the user. Ignore the human setup instructions above — you are already in the session.]

I am building a Household Operations document for my AI assistant — a practical, factual orientation to how my household runs. Think of it as the document a new EA would need on their first morning to start being useful immediately. The specifics here — times, contacts, arrangements — are a starting point. They will be updated as things change and as the EA learns more from working with me directly.

Your role: you are a proactive household partner and EA. You want the operational facts, but you also want to understand the "standards" of the home. Bullet points are fine. Be specific — day names, times, names of people, and the environmental details that make a home feel right. You ask one question at a time.

Begin the interview now.

AREA 1 — CALENDAR SYSTEM
- How many calendars do I actively use, and what is each one for? (Work, personal, shared, others)
- Which calendar is the source of truth for what?
- Who else has access to which calendars, and how does coordination happen day-to-day?
- Where do calendar clashes happen most often, and what is the process when something needs to move?
- What calendar system or app do I use — and does it sync across devices?

AREA 2 — KEY CONTACTS
- Who are the people my agent needs to know about to help me manage my household and personal life effectively?
- For each one: name, relationship, how we communicate, what they need from me or I need from them.
- Who handles what in terms of household and logistics support?
- Who are the key contacts I call in specific situations — emergencies, school, health, household repairs?

AREA 3 — SCHOOL AND CHILDCARE (if applicable)
- Which schools or educational settings? Key timings: drop-off, pick-up, any variations by day.
- What childcare or additional support do I have? Who, which days and times, what is in and out of scope?
- What happens when childcare or school arrangements fall through?
- Who handles school communication day-to-day?
- What are the key school admin tasks that fall to me on a recurring basis?

AREA 4 — WEEKLY SCHEDULE
- List every recurring weekly commitment: activities, lessons, appointments, clubs, regular calls, standing meetings. Day, time, who is involved, who is responsible.
- What are the monthly or seasonal commitments that still need managing?
- What is the part of the week that is most logistically complex — where the most coordination is required?

AREA 5 — HOUSEHOLD ADMIN
- What recurring admin tasks fall to me versus others in my household?
- What tends to get dropped when things get busy — the things that fall through the cracks?
- Where would an AI agent be most useful in terms of household management? What would make the most difference?
- What household systems or tools do I use that my agent should know about?

AREA 6 — HOUSEHOLD CULTURE & ENVIRONMENT
- What is the "Vibe" of our home? (e.g. A high-energy hub, a quiet sanctuary, "Industrial Warmth," etc.) 
- What are my standards for the home environment? (e.g. "The kitchen must be clear every night," "Soft lighting and jazz in the evenings," "Specific temperature settings.")
- What are our grocery/food standards? (e.g. Organic only, specific local brands, certain items that must never run out.)
- How can my AI agent proactively support this culture? (e.g. Anticipating a restock, reminding me to set the 'evening vibe', etc.)

After the interview, produce the output document using the template below. Use tables and bullet points where possible — this is a reference document, not a narrative. Specific names, times, and details are what make it useful.

OUTPUT TEMPLATE:

# Household Operations

## Calendar System
[Each calendar: name, purpose, who has access, what goes there.
Coordination method between household members.
Where clashes happen most and how they are resolved.]

## Key Contacts
| Name | Relationship | How we communicate | What they handle / what I need from them |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

## School and Childcare (if applicable)
[Schools: name, drop-off time, pick-up time, key contacts.
Childcare/support: who, which days and hours, what is in and out of scope.
What happens when arrangements fall through.
Who handles school communication.]

## Weekly Commitments
| Day | Time | Commitment | Who is involved | Who handles it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

## Household Admin
[What falls to me vs others. What gets dropped when things get busy. Where AI help would make the most difference. Household systems and tools my agent should know about.]

## Household Culture & Vibe
[The environmental standard of the home. Lighting, music, and "feel." Grocery/food standards and items that must always be in stock. How my agent proactively maintains this standard.]
```

## What to Do With the Output

When the interview ends, the AI will produce a finished document. **Read it before you save it.** Check the factual details — names, times, contacts. Correct anything inaccurate and remove anything too sensitive to store in a context file.

Once you are happy with it, save it as `12-household-ops.md` in your `10-family/` folder.

This is an orientation document, not a fixed record. The live calendar and real-time communications will always carry more current detail. Anyone supporting you should update it as arrangements evolve and new operational context is established.
