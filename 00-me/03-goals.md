# goals.md — What I Am Actually Building Toward

---

## How to Use This File

Copy everything inside the prompt block below and paste it into any AI of your choice — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi, or any other. The AI will interview you about your real priorities — not an aspirational list, but specific goals with actual stakes, honest obstacles, and real deadlines. When the interview is done, the AI will automatically produce a finished document.

**This file decays fast. It should be updated whenever priorities shift — by you, or by anyone supporting you who has learned something here worth capturing.**

---

## The Prompt

```
I am building a Goals document for my AI assistant — something that tells it what I am actually trying to accomplish — not an aspirational list I will never look at again, but a precise account of my real priorities with real stakes and real deadlines. When this file is done, any AI reading it should be able to give me advice that is calibrated to what I am actually trying to build, not a generic idea of success.

Your role: you are a trusted advisor — commercially sharp, analytically rigorous, and completely honest. You do not let me get away with vague intentions. When something sounds like what I think I should want rather than what I actually want, you name it and push me to be more specific. You challenge aspirational framing. You ask one question at a time and you do not move on until the answer is concrete.

Begin the interview now.

AREA 1 — THE BIG PICTURE
- If this year goes exactly right, what is materially different about my life by the end of it? Not a feeling — a fact.
- What would make this year feel like a genuine success? And what would make it feel like a failure — even if things looked fine from the outside?
- What is the single most important thing I am trying to accomplish right now — the one that, if it happened, would make everything else feel worthwhile?
- What is the theme or through-line of this period of my life?

AREA 2 — PROFESSIONAL AND WORK GOALS
- What are my top 1–3 professional priorities right now?
- For each one: what is the specific, concrete outcome that tells me I have achieved it? Not "grow my income" but "sign two new clients at X value by end of Q3."
- What needs to be true by the end of the next 90 days for me to feel on track?
- Which goal am I most excited about? Which one am I most likely to quietly let slide — and why?
- What am I actively avoiding because it feels risky, uncomfortable, or uncertain? Be honest.

AREA 3 — PERSONAL GOALS
- What do I want for my health, energy, and physical wellbeing?
- What do I want more of in my personal life that other things are currently crowding out?
- What habit or pattern am I genuinely trying to change? Build?
- What experience or milestone do I keep putting off that I actually want?
- Who do I want more of in my life — and who is quietly fading that I do not want to lose?

AREA 4 — THE HONEST LAYER AND ANTI-GOALS
(These questions usually reveal what actually matters.)
- Which goal am I most likely to self-sabotage — and what does that self-sabotage look like when it happens?
- What do I want this year that I would be embarrassed to say out loud?
- What would I regret not having done or tried by the end of this year?
- What is on my list that I do not actually care about? Why is it still there?
- **Anti-Goals:** What am I consciously choosing *not* to do this year, even if it feels tempting or urgent? 
- **The Sacrifice:** What am I giving up to make my top goals a reality? (e.g. time, a specific project, a certain level of social life, a professional opportunity). 

AREA 5 — OBSTACLES
- What is most likely to derail me from the things that matter most?
- What got in the way last year that I am at risk of repeating?
- What support, resource, or change in my own behaviour would make the biggest single difference?
- What will I need to say no to in order to protect the things I care about?

After the interview, produce the output document using the template below. Each priority must be concrete — "grow my practice" is not a goal, "sign one new retained client at £X/month by end of Q2" is a goal. Write it as a briefing for an advisor who needs to give me useful, calibrated guidance — not a motivational document.

OUTPUT TEMPLATE:

# My Goals — [Year/Quarter]

## This Period's Theme
[One sentence that captures what this period is really about — the throughline beneath the specific goals.]

## Top Priorities
[Maximum 3. For each:]

**[Priority name]**
- What success looks like: [specific and measurable]
- Deadline or milestone: [date or quarter]
- Most likely obstacle: [honest, not sanitised]
- What I am doing about it: [concrete action, not intention]

## Personal Goals
[Health · relationships · time · experiences · habits — specific, not aspirational. Include the ones that feel too small or too personal to say out loud.]

## The Honest Layer & Anti-Goals
[What I want but have not fully admitted. What I am avoiding. What I would regret not trying.
- **My Anti-Goals:** [The 3-5 things I am NOT doing this period to protect my focus.]
- **The Sacrifice:** [What I am consciously giving up to ensure success elsewhere.]]

## Known Obstacles
[What derails me · what I need · what I am not yet doing · what I need to say no to]

## Last Updated
[Date — review at least quarterly, or after any significant change in circumstances]
```

---

## What to Do With the Output

When the interview ends, the AI will produce a finished goals document. **Read it before you save it.** Check that the priorities are real — specific, honest, and yours. If anything reads as aspirational rather than actual, or if a goal is missing that matters, fix it now.

Once you are happy with it, save it as `03-goals.md` in your `00-me/` folder.

Return to it whenever priorities shift — not to measure yourself, but to keep the context current. Whoever is supporting you should update it when they learn something about your goals or direction that is not yet captured here.
