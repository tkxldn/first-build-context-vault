# 00-me/ — Who You Are

The foundation of your context vault. Five files that give any AI a deep, honest picture of who you are — how you think, how you write, what you are building toward, and how to work with you effectively.

Everything else in the vault builds on what is established here. Do these five files first, in order, before moving to any other folder.

| File | What it builds | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [01-identity.md](01-identity.md) | Your values, how you think, how you decide — your full autobiographical introduction | 35–45 min |
| [02-voice.md](02-voice.md) | How you write and speak — the fix for "it doesn't sound like me" | 30–45 min |
| [03-goals.md](03-goals.md) | Your real priorities this year, with success criteria and honest stakes | 25–35 min |
| [04-working-with-me.md](04-working-with-me.md) | How you work best, your patterns, what your agent should notice | 25–35 min |
| [05-life.md](05-life.md) | Your social world, relationships, interests, career arc, how you spend time | 25–35 min |

**Start with `01-identity.md`.** Everything builds on it.
