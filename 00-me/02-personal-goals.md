# My Personal Goals

---

## How to Use This File

Copy everything inside the prompt block below and paste it into any chatbot of your choice — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any other. The chatbot will interview you about your real personal priorities — not an aspirational list, but specific goals with actual stakes, honest obstacles, and real deadlines. When the interview is done, the chatbot will automatically produce a finished document.

Do this after `01-identity.md`. If you run a business, your commercial and revenue goals live separately in `23-business-goals.md`. This file covers your personal life — health, relationships, habits, experiences, and what you are actually building toward as a person.

**This file decays fast. Return to it whenever priorities shift — at minimum, quarterly.**

---

## The Prompt

Click the **copy** button in the top-right corner of the grey box below, then paste it into your chatbot.

```
I am building a Personal Goals document for my AI assistant — something that tells it what I am actually trying to accomplish in my personal life. Not an aspirational list. Not a vision board. A precise account of my real priorities — with real stakes, honest obstacles, and real deadlines.

Your role: you are a trusted advisor — warm, analytically sharp, and completely honest. You do not let me get away with vague intentions. When something sounds like what I think I should want rather than what I actually want, you name it and push for specifics. You ask one question at a time and you do not move on until the answer is concrete.

Begin the interview now.

AREA 1 — THE BIG PICTURE
- If this year goes exactly right for me personally, what is materially different about my life by the end of it? Not a feeling — a fact.
- What would make this year feel like a genuine personal success? And what would make it feel like a failure — even if things looked fine from the outside?
- What is the single most important thing I am trying to accomplish right now — the one that, if it happened, would make everything else feel worthwhile?
- What is the theme or through-line of this period of my life?

AREA 2 — PERSONAL GOALS
- What do I want for my health, energy, and physical wellbeing — specifically?
- What do I want more of in my personal life that other things are currently crowding out?
- What habit or pattern am I genuinely trying to change or build?
- What experience or milestone do I keep putting off that I actually want?
- Who do I want more of in my life — and who is quietly fading that I do not want to lose?

AREA 3 — THE HONEST LAYER AND ANTI-GOALS
(These questions usually reveal what actually matters.)
- Which goal am I most likely to self-sabotage — and what does that look like when it happens?
- What do I want this year that I would be embarrassed to say out loud?
- What would I regret not having done or tried by the end of this year?
- What is on my list that I do not actually care about? Why is it still there?
- Anti-Goals: What am I consciously choosing not to do this year, even if it feels tempting or urgent?
- The Sacrifice: What am I giving up to make my top priorities a reality?

AREA 4 — OBSTACLES
- What is most likely to derail me from the things that matter most personally?
- What got in the way last year that I am at risk of repeating?
- What support, resource, or change in my own behaviour would make the biggest single difference?
- What will I need to say no to in order to protect what I care about?

After the interview, produce the output document using the template below. Each priority must be concrete — "be healthier" is not a goal, "run three times a week and be in bed by midnight" is. Write it as a briefing for an advisor who needs to give me useful, calibrated guidance — not a motivational document.

OUTPUT TEMPLATE:

# My Personal Goals — [Year/Quarter]

## This Period's Theme
[One sentence capturing what this period is really about — the through-line beneath the specific goals.]

## Top Priorities
[Maximum 3. For each:]

**[Priority name]**
- What success looks like: [specific and measurable]
- Deadline or milestone: [date or quarter]
- Most likely obstacle: [honest, not sanitised]
- What I am doing about it: [concrete action, not intention]

## Personal Goals
[Health · relationships · time · experiences · habits — specific, not aspirational. Include the ones that feel too small or too personal to say out loud.]

## The Honest Layer and Anti-Goals
[What I want but have not fully admitted. What I am avoiding. What I would regret not trying.
- My Anti-Goals: [The things I am NOT doing this period to protect my focus.]
- The Sacrifice: [What I am consciously giving up to ensure success elsewhere.]]

## Known Obstacles
[What derails me · what I need · what I am not yet doing · what I need to say no to]

## Last Updated
[Date — review at least quarterly, or after any significant change in circumstances]
```

---

## What to Do With the Output

When the interview ends, the chatbot will produce a finished goals document. **Read it before you save it.** Check that the priorities are real — specific, honest, and yours. If anything reads as aspirational rather than actual, or if a goal is missing that matters, fix it now.

Once you are happy with it, save it as `02-personal-goals.md` in your `00-me/` folder.

Return to it whenever priorities shift — not to measure yourself, but to keep the context current.
