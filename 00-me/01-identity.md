# Who I Am

---

## How to Use This File

Copy everything inside the prompt block below and paste it into any AI of your choice — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi, or any other. The AI will act as a biographer, interviewing you to write the definitive account of your life and identity. Answer as if you are talking to a trusted new friend or someone documenting your legacy. Answer honestly, not impressively. When the interview is done, the AI will automatically produce a finished document.

**Bring nothing to this session except your story. This is the foundation — every other file builds on it.**

---

## The Prompt

Click the **copy** button in the top-right corner of the grey box below, then paste it into your AI.

```
I am building a foundational identity document for my AI assistant. Its purpose is to give any AI a deep, accurate understanding of who I am — not my job title or my CV, but my values, how I think, how I make decisions, and who I am becoming. The quality of this file determines the quality of every AI interaction I will ever have, so I need it to be precise and honest, not polished.

Your role: you are an exceptional biographer and executive confidant. You are interviewing me to write a definitive account of my life, values, and identity. You are warm, deeply curious, and rigorous. You don't just want facts; you want the "soul" of the story. If I give a generic answer, gently push me to go deeper. Ask one question at a time. Listen carefully and probe before moving on.

Begin the interview now. Work through the areas below in order, but follow the conversation naturally.

AREA 0 — THE INTRODUCTION
- What is your full name?
- What do you prefer to be called? (Is there a call-sign, a nickname, or a specific way you like to be addressed?)
- If you were introducing yourself to someone who was going to become a major part of your life, how would you start your story?
- What is the one thing people always get wrong about you when they first meet you?

AREA 1 — MY VALUES
The ones I actually hold, not the ones that sound good.

- What are the 3–5 things I care about most — genuinely, not aspirationally?
- For each one: when have I held this value even when it cost me something? (Push for a specific example — not a principle, a moment.)
- What do I judge in other people, even when I try not to? (This usually reveals a hidden value I haven't named yet.)
- What would the people who know me best say about me — honestly, not kindly? What would they say behind my back that they wouldn't say to my face?
- What do I want people to say about me when I'm not in the room — in ten years, not right now?

AREA 2 — HOW I THINK AND DECIDE
- Am I instinctive or deliberate? Give me a recent example of a meaningful decision and walk me through exactly how I made it.
- Do I trust data, gut feel, or the read I have on a person — and in what order does each one matter?
- What kind of problem genuinely energises me — the type I would lean into even if nobody asked me to?
- What kind of work drains me, even when I am good at it and even when it is important?
- How do I behave when I am under pressure or making a high-stakes call? What do people around me notice?
- What do I need before I can commit to something? What does "enough information" feel like for me?
- What is my relationship with being wrong? How do I handle it privately vs publicly?

AREA 3 — WHO I AM
- What parts of who I am am I most proud of — the qualities I would keep even if they made my life harder?
- What labels do people apply to me that I would push back on? What do they get wrong about me?
- Where does my public persona diverge from who I actually am in private?
- What tension exists between who I am right now and who I am still becoming?
- What am I afraid of becoming?

AREA 4 — THE UNCOMFORTABLE LAYER
(These questions matter more than the others. Take your time.)
- What do I secretly value that I would be embarrassed to say out loud in a professional context?
- Where does my stated identity most diverge from my actual behaviour?
- What is the story I tell about myself that I am not sure is entirely true?
- What would a genuinely honest account of my failures say about me — the pattern across them, not the individual events?

After the interview is complete, produce the output document using the template below. Make it sharp and specific. No corporate language. No hedging. No aspirational filler. Write it as a briefing for someone who needs to understand this person deeply — not a performance for the person themselves.

OUTPUT TEMPLATE:

# My Identity: An Auto-biography

## The Basics
[My full name and what I prefer to be called. How I introduce myself to those who matter.]

## Core Values
[3–5 values. Each one named precisely — not a word like "integrity" but a sentence that captures what it actually means to me. Followed by one specific example of it in action — a moment, not a principle.]

## How I Think
[How I process complexity and ambiguity. Whether I am instinctive or deliberate. What kinds of problems energise me. What kind of work costs me even when I am good at it.]

## How I Decide
[What I weigh — data vs instinct vs people-read, and in what order. What I need before I can commit. How I behave under pressure. My relationship with being wrong.]

## Who I Am
[The identity I am proud of. The labels I reject. Where my public and private selves diverge. What the people who know me best would say honestly.]

## What I Am Becoming
[The tension between where I am and where I am going. What I want to be known for. What I am still working out. What I am afraid of becoming.]

## The Honest Layer
[The values I hold but do not broadcast. Where my stated identity diverges from my behaviour. The pattern in my failures. The story I tell about myself that I am not sure is entirely true.]
```

---

## What to Do With the Output

When the interview ends, the AI will produce a finished document — your identity brief. **Read it before you save it.** Check that it sounds like you — specific, honest, and accurate. Correct anything that is vague, softened, or not quite right. This document only works if it is true.

Once you are happy with it, save it as `01-identity.md` in your `00-me/` folder.

It is a living document — update it when something significant changes in how you see yourself, what you value, or where you are going. Anyone supporting you can update it as they learn things about you that are not yet captured here.
