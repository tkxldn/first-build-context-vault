# 30-agent/ — The Agent You Are Building

These are the last two files — and they are different from everything that came before.

The previous files were all about you. These files are about the personal AI agent you are building. Not a chatbot you use occasionally — a dedicated assistant that reads your entire context vault before every conversation, knows your name, operates by your rules, and gets better the longer you work with it.

You will use your chatbot (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini) to complete these files in the same way as the others — copy the prompt, paste it in, answer the questions. What you are defining, though, is not another document about yourself. You are defining the character and the operating rules of the agent that will use all of those documents.

Most people cannot answer these questions well until they have thought through their identity, their work, their household, and their goals. That is why these files come last.

| File | What it builds | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [31-my-agent.md](31-my-agent.md) | The character of your agent — its name, personality, values, how it communicates, what it notices proactively, and what it would never do. Once you have chosen a name, rename this file to match. | 30–40 min |
| [32-rules.md](32-rules.md) | How your agent operates — what it can do on its own initiative, what it must always check with you first, and what is never its call regardless of the circumstances | 25–35 min |

Do `31-my-agent.md` first. Rules without character are hollow — you need to know who your agent is before you can define how it should behave.
