# 30-agent/ — The AI You Are Building

These are the last two files in the vault — and they are different from everything that came before.

The previous files were about you. These files are about the AI you want working with you. Not its technical settings — its character, its name, its personality, how it communicates, and the rules it operates by.

Most people cannot answer these questions well until they have thought through their identity, their work, their household, and their goals. That is why these files come last. Do them after completing the other folders.

| File | What it builds | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [31-my-agent.md](31-my-agent.md) | The character of your agent — its name, personality, values, how it communicates, what it notices, and what it would never do. Once you have chosen a name, rename this file to match. | 30–40 min |
| [32-rules.md](32-rules.md) | How your agent operates — what it can do on its own initiative, what it must always check with you first, and what is never its call regardless of the circumstances | 25–35 min |

Do `31-my-agent.md` first. Rules without character are hollow — you need to know who your agent is before you can define how it should behave.
