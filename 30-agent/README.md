# 30-agent/ — The Agent You Are Building

These two files define the AI you want. Not its technical settings — its character, its personality, its values, and the rules it operates by.

Do these after completing the first nine files. You need to have thought through your identity, your work, your life, and your household before you can clearly define the agent you want supporting all of it. This is where the vault becomes personal: not just a briefing for any AI, but the beginning of a genuine working relationship with an AI that knows who it is.

| File | What it builds | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [31-my-agent.md](31-my-agent.md) | Character brief — name, personality, values, aesthetic, how it communicates, what it would never do. Rename to your agent's name once chosen. | 30–40 min |
| [32-rules.md](32-rules.md) | Operating rules — what it can do independently, what it must always check with you first, what is never its call | 25–35 min |

Do `31-my-agent.md` first. Rules without character are hollow — you need to know who your agent is before you can define how it should operate.
