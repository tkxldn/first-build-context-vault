# 20-business/ — Your Work

Not a CV. Not a bio. The honest picture of your professional world — what you actually do, how you work, who you work with, where you have come from, and where you are going.

Three files, each covering a distinct layer. Complete each one by copying the prompt, pasting it into your chatbot, and answering its questions.

| File | What it captures | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [21-work.md](21-work.md) | The day-to-day reality of your work — what you do, how you structure your time, who you work with, what energises and drains you, and the standard you hold yourself to | 30–45 min |
| [22-relationships.md](22-relationships.md) | How you build and maintain professional relationships — your network, how trust develops with you, how you stay connected, and what tends to fall away when you get busy | 25–35 min |
| [23-career.md](23-career.md) | Your career arc and trajectory — where you have come from, what your current career structure looks like (corporate, fractional, portfolio, hybrid, or in transition), and where you are building toward | 35–45 min |

Do them in order. `21-work.md` establishes the current reality; `22-relationships.md` builds on that professional context; `23-career.md` zooms out to the longer arc and direction.
