# How I Work

---

## How to Use This File

Copy everything inside the prompt block below and paste it into any AI of your choice — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any other. The AI will interview you about your professional life — what you do day-to-day, how you work, who you work with, and what energises and drains you. When the interview is done, the AI will automatically produce a finished document.

This file covers the texture of how you work right now. Your career arc, structure, and trajectory live in `21-career.md`.

---

## The Prompt

Click the **copy** button in the top-right corner of the grey box below, then paste it into your AI.

```
[For agents running a vault build session: conduct this interview directly with the user. Ignore the human setup instructions above — you are already in the session.]

I am building a Work context document for my AI assistant — something that gives it a deep, honest understanding of my professional life. What I actually do, how I work, who I work with or for, what is going well, and where it is heading. The kind of picture a trusted advisor who has spent a year working alongside me would have.

Your role: you are that trusted advisor — commercially sharp, curious about the real texture of my work, and completely honest. You do not let me get away with elevator pitch answers. When something is vague or sounds like a polished version of reality, you ask me to get specific. You ask one question at a time.

The questions below cover different possible professional situations — employment, running a business, portfolio work, and everything in between. Answer what is relevant to your situation. Skip what is not.

Begin the interview now.

AREA 1 — WHAT I DO
- How do I describe what I do to someone I have just met — not the formal version, the honest one?
- What problem do I actually solve for the people I work with or for, in the terms they would use?
- What do I do that someone more junior, less experienced, or in a different role genuinely could not?
- What is my positioning or reputation — what am I known for, and what do people come to me specifically for?
- What do I consistently deliver that people did not expect when they first started working with me?

AREA 2 — HOW MY PROFESSIONAL LIFE WORKS
- What does my professional structure look like right now — employed, self-employed, running a business, portfolio, something hybrid?
- What does a typical working week look like when things are going well? How do I structure my time?
- What do I personally spend the most time on — and is that the highest-value use of my time?
- What would fall apart or fall behind if I disappeared for two weeks?
- What is the work I find most satisfying, and what is the work I find most costly — even when I am good at it?

AREA 3 — WHO I WORK WITH OR FOR
- Walk me through my current professional situation: who I work with, what I am delivering or responsible for, what is going well, what is complex.
- For each key relationship — client, employer, partner, stakeholder: how do we communicate, how often do we interact, what does success look like?
- What is the thing I am most concerned about across my current professional commitments?
- Who do I need to keep well — the relationships that require the most active management?

AREA 4 — WHAT ENERGISES AND DRAINS ME PROFESSIONALLY
- What kind of problem or project genuinely excites me — the type I would lean into even without being asked?
- What type of work do I do well but find costly over time?
- What would I stop doing immediately if I could?
- What kind of work or professional relationship do I want more of?

AREA 5 — THE SOUL OF MY WORK
- What is the non-negotiable standard I hold myself to — the thing I would not compromise even under pressure?
- What do I want clients, colleagues, or collaborators to say about working with me — not the impressive version, the true one?
- What does genuinely good work look like to me? What is the bar?

After the interview, produce the output document using the template below. Be specific about the actual texture of my work — this should read like a briefing from someone who knows my professional world well, not a bio or a CV summary.

OUTPUT TEMPLATE:

# My Work

## What I Do
[One or two sentences capturing my professional reality — what I actually do and what problem I solve. What I am known for. What I deliver that surprises people.]

## How I Work
[My professional structure — employed, self-employed, other. How I spend my time. What a good week looks like. What I spend the most time on versus what is highest value.]

## Current Professional Situation
[The honest current picture — who I work with or for, what I am delivering, what is going well, what is complex. One section per significant relationship or commitment:]

- What I am responsible for or delivering:
- How we communicate and how often:
- What is going well:
- What is complex or requires active management:
- What success looks like:

## What Energises vs Drains Me
[The work that genuinely excites me. The work I do well but find costly. What I would stop if I could. What I want more of.]

## The Soul of My Work
[The non-negotiable standard I hold myself to. What I want to be known for by the people who work with me. What genuinely good work looks like to me.]
```

## What to Do With the Output

When the interview ends, the AI will produce a finished document. **Read it before you save it.** Check that the picture of your professional life is accurate and honest — not the polished version. Correct anything that undersells the complexity or oversimplifies what you actually do.

Once you are happy with it, save it as `24-how-i-work.md` in your `20-business/` folder.

Update it whenever your professional situation materially changes — new role, new clients, new direction. Anyone supporting you should keep it current as they learn more about the texture of your work.
