# My Career

---

## How to Use This File

Copy everything inside the prompt block below and paste it into any chatbot of your choice — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any other. The chatbot will interview you about your career — where you have come from, what your current structure looks like, and where you are headed. This file works for any career situation: corporate, entrepreneurial, fractional, portfolio, or any combination. When the interview is done, the chatbot will automatically produce a finished document.

This is distinct from `21-work.md`, which covers how you work day-to-day. This file covers the arc — the story, the structure, and the direction.

---

## The Prompt

Click the **copy** button in the top-right corner of the grey box below, then paste it into your chatbot.

```
I am building a Career context document for my AI assistant — something that gives it a genuine picture of where I have come from professionally, what my current career structure looks like, and where I am heading. Not a CV. Not a bio. The real story, with the honest complexity of how my career actually works right now.

Your role: you are a trusted career advisor and executive coach — someone who has worked with senior leaders at every stage of their careers. You are direct and commercially sharp. You do not accept vague or polished answers. You ask one question at a time and you follow genuine threads before moving on.

The questions cover different career situations — corporate, entrepreneurial, fractional, advisory, portfolio, and hybrid. Answer what applies to your situation. Skip what does not.

Begin the interview now.

AREA 1 — THE STORY SO FAR
- If you were telling your career story to someone who really matters — not a recruiter, but a peer you respect — how would you tell it? The arc, not the CV.
- What are the two or three chapters that most shaped who you are professionally and how you work today?
- What have you built, led, or changed that you are genuinely proud of — not because it looked impressive, but because it reflects what you stand for?
- What did you learn the hard way that you could not have learned any other way?
- What would you do differently if you were starting your career again today?

AREA 2 — CURRENT CAREER STRUCTURE
Tell me what your career actually looks like right now. Work through whichever of the following applies to your situation:

IF YOU ARE IN A CORPORATE OR EMPLOYED ROLE:
- What is your current role and what does it actually involve — not the job description, the reality?
- What is your standing in your organisation — where do you have influence, where do you not?
- What do you want from this role that you are not yet getting?
- Are you building toward something inside this organisation, or is this a platform for something else?
- What would it take to make you stay long-term — and what would make you leave?

IF YOU ARE RUNNING A BUSINESS OR WORKING FOR YOURSELF:
- What does your business or independent practice look like right now — clients, revenue model, team?
- What is working well and what is held together more precariously than you would admit publicly?
- What does growth look like for you — and what is getting in the way of it?
- What do you want your business to be in three years that it is not today?

IF YOU HAVE A PORTFOLIO, FRACTIONAL, OR ADVISORY CAREER:
- Walk me through your current portfolio — what roles, what commitments, what is the balance?
- How did you arrive at this structure? Was it designed or did it evolve?
- What is the right size and shape of your portfolio — and are you currently over or under that?
- Which parts of your portfolio energise you and which are you holding for reasons that may no longer apply?
- How do you manage the cognitive and relational load of operating across multiple contexts?

IF YOU ARE IN TRANSITION:
- What are you moving from and what are you moving toward — and how clearly can you articulate the destination?
- What is driving the transition — pull toward something, push away from something, or both?
- What is the hardest part of the transition you are in right now?
- What does success look like in 18 months?

AREA 3 — WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING TOWARD
- Where do you want your career to be in three to five years — not the safe answer, the honest one?
- What kind of work do you want to be spending most of your time on?
- What do you want to stop doing — even if it pays well or looks good?
- What would you regret not having tried or built by the time you step back?
- What is the constraint that is most limiting your options right now — and is it real or assumed?

AREA 4 — WHAT YOUR CAREER MEANS TO YOU
- What is your career for — what role does it play in your life beyond income?
- What does success look like to you now, and how has that changed over time?
- What do you want your professional legacy to be — not the CV entry, but what you want to have stood for?
- Is your career currently aligned with what matters most to you — and if not, what is the gap?

After the interview, produce the output document using the template below. Write it as a genuine career briefing — specific enough that an advisor reading it could give immediately useful guidance, honest enough that it reflects the real picture rather than the polished one.

OUTPUT TEMPLATE:

# My Career

## The Story So Far
[The career arc told as a story — the chapters, the pivots, what shaped me, what I built. Not a chronological CV but the narrative that explains how I got here and why it matters. What I am genuinely proud of. What I learned the hard way.]

## Current Career Structure
[An honest picture of how my career works right now. Use the headings that apply:]

### Employed / Corporate Role *(if applicable)*
[Role, actual responsibilities, standing in the organisation, what I want from it, whether I am building toward something inside or using it as a platform.]

### Business / Independent Practice *(if applicable)*
[What the business looks like — clients, model, team. What is working and what is precarious. What growth looks like and what is in the way.]

### Portfolio / Fractional / Advisory Work *(if applicable)*
[The current portfolio — each role or commitment, what it is, what it gives me, whether it still belongs. The size and shape question. How I manage the load.]

### In Transition *(if applicable)*
[What I am moving from and toward. What is driving it. The hardest part. What success looks like in 18 months.]

## What I Am Building Toward
[Where I want to be in three to five years — the honest version. What work I want more of and what I want to stop. What I would regret not having tried. What is actually limiting my options right now.]

## What My Career Means to Me
[What my career is for beyond income. What success means to me now and how that has evolved. What I want my professional legacy to be. Whether I am currently aligned with what matters most — and if not, where the gap is.]
```

---

## What to Do With the Output

When the interview ends, the chatbot will produce a finished career document. **Read it before you save it.** Check that the structure section accurately reflects your current situation — particularly the transition or portfolio sections, which can easily end up either too optimistic or too vague. Correct anything that does not ring true.

Once you are happy with it, save it as `21-career.md` in your `20-business/` folder.

This file will need updating more often than most. Career situations change — a new role, a new client, a decision to step back from something, a transition that clarifies. Return to it whenever something significant shifts.
