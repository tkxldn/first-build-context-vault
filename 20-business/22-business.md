# My Business

---

## How to Use This File

This file is for people who run something of their own — a business, a consultancy practice, a freelance operation, a portfolio of clients, or anything where you are responsible for building and sustaining it rather than just working within it.

If that does not apply to you, skip this file entirely.

Copy everything inside the prompt block below and paste it into any chatbot of your choice — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any other. The chatbot will interview you about your business — what it is, how it works, what is live right now, and where it is going. When the interview is done, the chatbot will automatically produce a finished document.

Do this after `21-career.md`. The career file establishes where you have come from. This file covers the business you have built or are building.

---

## The Prompt

Click the **copy** button in the top-right corner of the grey box below, then paste it into your chatbot.

```
I am building a Business context document for my AI assistant — something that gives it a deep, honest understanding of the business I run. Not a pitch deck. Not a website bio. The real picture: what the business actually is, how it works day to day, what is going well, what is not, and where I am taking it.

Your role: you are a trusted advisor and strategic partner who has just agreed to spend the next year working closely with me on my business. You are commercially sharp, direct, and genuinely curious. You do not accept vague or polished answers. You ask one question at a time. When something sounds like an elevator pitch rather than reality, you push for specifics.

Begin the interview now.

AREA 1 — WHAT THE BUSINESS IS
- What does my business do — in one honest sentence, not the version I use for networking?
- What problem do I solve, and for whom? Be specific about who the client actually is.
- How long have I been running this, and what stage is it at right now?
- How do I make money — what is the actual revenue model?
- What do clients or customers get from me that they could not easily get elsewhere?

AREA 2 — HOW IT WORKS RIGHT NOW
- Walk me through the current state of the business — clients, pipeline, revenue, team or no team. The honest picture.
- What does my week actually look like when the business is running well?
- What do I personally spend most of my time on — and is that the best use of my time?
- What would fall apart or go backwards if I stepped away for a month?
- What is held together more precariously than I would admit publicly?

AREA 3 — CLIENTS AND REVENUE
- Who are my current clients or customers? What do I do for each of them?
- How do new clients or customers typically find me? What does the pipeline look like?
- What does the revenue picture look like — stable, growing, lumpy, seasonal?
- Which clients or revenue streams matter most? Which are I keeping for reasons that may no longer apply?
- What is the client or customer I most want more of — and what is stopping me from getting there?

AREA 4 — WHAT IS WORKING AND WHAT IS NOT
- What are the two or three things the business does genuinely well right now?
- What is the biggest problem or constraint in the business at the moment?
- What have I tried that has not worked — and what did I learn from it?
- What am I avoiding or deferring that I know I should be addressing?
- What is the honest reason things are not further along than they are?

AREA 5 — WHERE IT IS GOING
- What does this business look like in two to three years if things go well?
- What would I need to change — about the business, about how I run it, about my own role — to get there?
- What do I want to stop doing within the business, even if it currently pays?
- Is there an exit, a succession, a scale play, or am I building something I want to run indefinitely? What does success actually look like for me with this?
- What is the one constraint that, if removed, would change everything?

AREA 6 — THE HONEST LAYER
- What do I secretly want for this business that I have not fully committed to out loud?
- What am I most afraid of with this business?
- What would I do differently if I were starting again today?
- What is the gap between how I describe this business publicly and what I actually experience running it?

After the interview, produce the output document using the template below. Write it as a genuine business briefing — specific enough that a new strategic advisor could get up to speed immediately. Honest enough that it reflects the real picture, not the polished one.

OUTPUT TEMPLATE:

# My Business

## What It Is
[One or two sentences — what the business does, what problem it solves, who for, and how it makes money. The honest version, not the pitch.]

## Current State
[The real picture right now — clients, revenue, team, pipeline. What a good week looks like. What I spend most of my time on. What would fall apart if I stepped away.]

## Clients and Revenue
[Who the clients or customers are and what I deliver for them. How new business comes in. The revenue picture — stable, growing, lumpy. Which relationships and revenue streams matter most.]

## What Is Working and What Is Not
[The genuine strengths. The biggest current constraint or problem. What I have tried that has not worked. What I am avoiding that I should not be.]

## Where It Is Going
[The two-to-three year picture if things go well. What needs to change to get there. What I want to stop doing. What success looks like for me with this business — exit, scale, sustain, or something else.]

## The Honest Layer
[What I want but have not fully committed to. What I am most afraid of. What I would do differently. The gap between how I describe it publicly and what I actually experience.]
```

---

## What to Do With the Output

When the interview ends, the chatbot will produce a finished business document. **Read it before you save it.** Pay particular attention to the "What Is Not Working" and "Honest Layer" sections — these are the ones most likely to come out softened. If they read too polished, go back and sharpen them. An agent briefed on a sanitised version of your business will give you sanitised advice.

Once you are happy with it, save it as `22-business.md` in your `20-business/` folder.

Return to this file whenever something significant changes — a major new client, a revenue shift, a decision about direction. It will need updating more often than most.
