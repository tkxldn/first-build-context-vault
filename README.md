# Your AI Context Vault

Eleven interview-first prompts that give any AI a complete, honest briefing on who you are — before it does a single piece of work for you.

Each file contains one prompt. Copy it, paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any AI you use. The AI interviews you one question at a time, then automatically produces a finished document. Save it. That document becomes a permanent context file.

When you load these files into any AI conversation, the quality of every output changes immediately — more specific, more calibrated, and genuinely sounding like you rather than a generic assistant.

---

## How It Works

1. Open a file below
2. Read the short instructions at the top — they tell you what to prepare
3. Find the section marked **"The Prompt"** and click the **copy** button in the top-right corner of the grey box
4. Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any AI
5. Answer the questions conversationally — talk, do not fill in a form
6. When the interview ends, the AI produces a finished document automatically
7. Read it, correct anything that is not quite right, and save it with the filename shown at the bottom

**The most important rule:** be specific. Vague context produces vague output.

- "I value quality" — useless
- "When speed conflicts with quality, quality wins every time. I would rather deliver late than send something I am embarrassed by" — a briefing

**Time per file:** 20–45 minutes. Spread them across several sittings — not all at once. The thinking time between files is part of the process.

## How to Load These Files Into Any AI

At the start of any conversation, share the files relevant to the task:

> "Before we begin, read these context files. They tell you who I am, how I work, and what I need from you. Apply everything in them from your first response."

Start with the `00-me/` files. Add the others as the task requires. The difference in output quality is immediate.

---

## A Note on Privacy

Never share passwords, bank details, or granular financial data with any cloud-based AI. Focus on intent, decision logic, values, and context — these are what calibrate the AI and what make every output better.

If a prompt asks for something you are not comfortable sharing, skip it or describe it in general terms. The files work at whatever level of detail you are comfortable with.
